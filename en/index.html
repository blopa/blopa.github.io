    Hi, I'm Pablo Montenegro

    Full-stack developer based in Amsterdam, born and raised in Rio de Janeiro. I build things because something annoyed me, and then I write about it here, twice: once in English and once in Portuguese. I've been doing that since 2008, with a few long naps in between.
